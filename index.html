WHO WE ARE

Our Church Mission & Vision

United in one heart and purpose, our mission and vision reflect who we are, what we believe, and the calling God has entrusted to us.

Our Vision

To see thousands of people and families whose lives are changed and transformed by the Gospel of the Lord Jesus Christ in the power of the Holy Spirit through passionate evangelism and intentional discipleship.

Our Mission

To know God through His Word, to glorify God through life and service, and to share Jesus through evangelism and discipleship.

OUR JOURNEY

A Short History of Our Church

New Life Baptist Church began with a simple gathering in a rented home, where a small group of believers came together for Bible study and fellowship. What started as a humble Bible study became the beginning of a growing ministry, united by a desire to know God's Word, share the Gospel, and see lives changed through Jesus Christ.

As the ministry continued to grow, God faithfully opened doors and provided opportunities for the church to reach more individuals and families. From those humble beginnings, New Life Baptist Church has continued to stand on God's Word, committed to prayer, evangelism, discipleship, and faithful service. Today, the church continues to build lives, reach the community, and point the next generation to the new life found in Jesus Christ.

A Testimony of God's Goodness and Faithfulness in Our Church

Our Ministries

Dicipleship Ministry

Our Discipleship Ministries help believers grow deeper in their relationship with Jesus Christ through God’s Word, prayer, fellowship, and intentional discipleship. We seek to equip every believer to live out their faith, serve others, and make disciples who will continue the work of Christ.

Music Ministry

The Music Ministry is a place where musicians and singers can use their God-given talents to glorify Him through singing, playing instruments, leading congregational worship, and serving in the choir. We encourage one another to grow not only in our musical abilities but also in our knowledge of the Lord Jesus Christ and His Word. Through music and worship, we seek to honor God, encourage the church, and lead others closer to Him.

Children's Ministry

Christ's Alpha Squad Ministry is a place where kids explore Bible stories, sing, and engage in creative activities, all while growing in their faith and learning to love God and others.

Youth Ministry

SIDLAC Youth Ministry, an acronym for "Shining In Darkness, Living after Christ," is a welcoming family for young people seeking connection to Christ through soul winning, growing faith in Christ through service, and joy with Christ though fellowship and youth events.

Evangelism Ministry

The Evangelism Ministry is committed to fulfilling Christ’s command in Matthew 28:19–20 (KJV), “Go ye therefore, and teach all nations.” Through outreach, personal evangelism, and sharing God’s Word, we seek to lead people to Jesus Christ and help them discover the new life found in Him.
